How to play

You're the release engineer. Each level is a config change that may or may not be broken. Get it to the whole fleet if it's good, or roll it back if it's bad, while hurting as few requests as possible.

  1. Review. Read the ticket and the config diff. The co-pilot on the right posts a pre-flight review.
  2. Plan. Choose a strategy (big bang, fast or staged), a canary datacenter that gets the change first, and how long each stage bakes.
  3. Deploy and watch. Each stage applies the config, bakes, then runs a health check. If any datacenter goes over 10% errors, the health gate rolls back on its own.
  4. Decide. After each stage it's your call: proceed or roll back. The gate only catches big failures, so compare with the baseline and ask the co-pilot ("why is SYD elevated?"). You can also type deploy, proceed, rollback, status or review.
  5. Fail stale (top bar). Datacenters validate new configs and keep serving the last known-good one if validation fails.

Scoring. Bad changes must end rolled back; good changes must reach 100%. The grade comes from the blast radius, and big-bang deploys cap at B: getting lucky isn't engineering.
